All figures of merit

An enemy search radar rotates, so its beam points at us only briefly ( red flashes). Our receiver visits that frequency at a fixed rhythm (ticks). We only catch the radar when a visit lands on a flash ( green). If the two rhythms line up badly, the visits always land between flashes. That is lock-out. Changing the rhythm slightly fixes it.

Intercept time vs receiver revisit period

Red bars: chance of lock-out, where gcd(Tr, Ts) > τr+τs−1 and some timings are never intercepted. Green line: optimal revisit period.
